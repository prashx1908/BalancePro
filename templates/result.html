Your work-life balance is currently poor and requires immediate attention. Work has become the dominant force in your life, regularly overshadowing personal needs, relationships, and well-being. Recommendation: This situation requires both immediate relief measures and longer-term structural changes:

Disable work notifications on your phone during personal time. Schedule at least 30 minutes daily for a stress-reducing activity you enjoy.

Develop strategies to increase efficiency during work hours rather than extending work time. Prioritize sleep hygiene and regular meals. Gradually reintroduce neglected hobbies or relationships.

Remember that improving a poor work-life balance takes time and consistent effort. Start with small, manageable changes and build upon successes gradually. Your health and well-being are worth the investment required to create sustainable balance.
